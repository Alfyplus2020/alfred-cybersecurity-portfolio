# Cybersecurity & AI Governance in the Canadian Hospitality and Tourism Industry (Capstone — In Progress)

**Master's Capstone Research Project — University of Guelph, Cybersecurity and Threat Intelligence**
**Status: IN PROGRESS — literature review stage · under faculty supervision**

## Research Focus

This capstone examines how the Canadian hospitality and tourism industry currently approaches
cybersecurity and AI adoption, specifically:

- How the sector currently views and prioritizes cybersecurity risk
- How (and whether) organizations in this space are championing **AI security** practices as they
  adopt AI-driven tools and services
- What their experience has been so far in balancing AI adoption against security and governance
  concerns
- How they're thinking about the future of both domains — cybersecurity and AI governance —
  specifically within a hospitality/tourism operating context (guest data, booking systems,
  property management platforms, AI-driven guest service tools, etc.)

## Methodology (Planned)

- **Literature review** (current stage): surveying existing research on cybersecurity maturity and
  AI governance adoption in hospitality/tourism, both in Canada and comparable markets
- **Primary data collection**: semi-structured interviews with Canadian hospitality and tourism
  businesses, to capture real operational experience and forward-looking views directly from
  industry — not just what's published

## Why this project

This capstone sits directly at the intersection of my current specialization — **AI governance and
AI security within a GRC framework** — and a sector that is adopting AI-driven guest and operational
tools quickly, often without a mature security or governance posture to match. It's also a natural
extension of the GRC consulting direction I'm building toward: understanding how a specific,
under-studied industry actually experiences and governs AI risk, grounded in real interviews rather
than only desk research.
